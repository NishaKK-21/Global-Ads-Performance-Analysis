Global Digital Advertising Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes global digital advertising campaign performance using Microsoft Power BI. The analysis focuses on revenue, advertising spend, Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), campaign types, and country-wise performance.

The dashboard helps compare Google Ads, Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads to identify trends and gain useful business insights.

Dataset

Source: Kaggle

Dataset: Global Ads Performance Dataset

Records: 1,800

Period: 2024

Platforms: Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads

Project Objectives

Analyze advertising revenue and spending.

Compare the performance of different advertising platforms.

Evaluate ROAS across campaign types.

Analyze monthly revenue trends.

Compare advertising performance across countries.

Present insights through an interactive dashboard.

Tools and Technologies

Microsoft Power BI

Power Query for data cleaning and transformation

DAX for calculations and measures

Data Analysis and Visualization

Interactive Dashboard Design

Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

KPI Cards: Total Revenue and Overall ROAS.

Monthly Revenue Trend: Analyzes revenue changes over time.

Revenue by Platform: Compares revenue across advertising platforms.

Ad Spend vs Revenue: Compares advertising expenditure with generated revenue.

ROAS by Campaign Type: Evaluates advertising efficiency across campaign types.

Revenue by Country: Displays country-wise advertising revenue.

Revenue Share by Campaign Type: Shows each campaign type's contribution to total revenue.

Interactive Slicers: Filter results by platform and date.

Tooltip Pages: Display additional information about clicks and Cost per Acquisition (CPA).

Key Findings

Total revenue was approximately 54.18 million.

Overall ROAS was approximately 4.88.

Google Ads generated the highest total revenue.

TikTok Ads achieved the highest platform-level ROAS, at approximately 7.62.

Search campaigns had the highest ROAS among campaign types, at approximately 5.31.

December recorded the highest monthly revenue.

India had the highest country-level ROAS, at approximately 5.50.
